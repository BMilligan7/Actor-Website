I’m a 24-Year-Old Actor:
What the Hell Am I Doing With My Life?

Honest, unfiltered, and probably familiar to anyone who’s ever tried to figure it out while actually living it.

Welcome, friends.

This is a messy one. Honest, unfiltered, and probably familiar to anyone who’s ever tried to “figure it out” while actually living it.

I’m 24 years old, and I’m an actor. And right now, I feel lost.

Not in a poetic, romantic way. In a real, uncomfortable, lying-in-my-childhood-bedroom-at-my-parents’-house kind of way.

I flew back to the East Coast for a couple of weeks, and instead of feeling grounded, I feel like I’ve hit pause on a life that was already confusing to begin with.

I’m tired. Not just “I need a nap” tired. I mean drained—mentally, physically, emotionally.

And I keep asking myself the same question over and over:

What the hell am I doing with my life?

Acting, and the Slow Fade of Certainty

I’ve been pursuing acting full-time for a little over a year now.

And I won’t lie—it’s been hard.

There was a time when I felt more certain. More driven. More hungry. I used to feel like I was building toward something undeniable.

But recently, that feeling has started to fade.

I just came back from the International Black Theatre Festival, where I performed a monologue in front of a talent agent. I went in thinking, this could be it. The moment. The break. The door opening.

Instead, I left feeling… unsure.

I used a monologue I had worked on before, something I’d even shaped with AI assistance. In rehearsal, it had felt grounded and real. I could connect to it. I believed it.

But in front of the agent?

It didn’t land.

And worse than that—it didn’t even feel real to me.

That’s the part that stuck with me.

Because if I don’t believe it, why would anyone else?

I walked away questioning not just the performance, but myself. My ability. My instincts. My path.

And I’ve been sitting with that ever since.

1. My Body Feels Off, and I Can’t Ignore It Anymore

I’ve always been athletic. I was a bodybuilder in college. I used to feel strong, sharp, in control of my body.

Now? Not so much.

I still look “fit” by most standards, but I don’t feel like myself when I look in the mirror. I feel heavier. Slower. Off.

I’ve been dealing with injuries. I’m not sleeping well. Stress is constant. My schedule doesn’t really allow for recovery, and I keep pushing anyway.

And I’m starting to realize something I’ve been avoiding:

My habits are catching up to me.

I feel like I’m constantly choosing between my health and my ambition, and neither side is winning.

I want to take care of myself. I want to feel good in my body again. I want energy. I want clarity.

But I also feel like I’m always chasing something just out of reach, and that chase is costing me more than I expected.

And then there’s the financial reality of it all.

Acting isn’t paying the bills consistently. And that pressure changes how everything feels.

Even basic things—like eating well, resting, living in a stable environment—start to feel like luxuries instead of foundations.

I know that mindset isn’t fully true, but it’s the emotional reality I’ve been living in.

2. The Thought I Keep Avoiding: A Corporate Job

Lately, I’ve been thinking about something I never thought I’d seriously consider:

Getting a full-time corporate job.

Not because I’ve given up on acting. But because I’m wondering if I need stability. Structure. A reset.

The idea would be simple: work, save money, breathe a little, build something on the side, and figure things out from a more grounded place.

And honestly?

Part of me is curious.

I’ve never actually tried that kind of life. I’ve always assumed it wasn’t for me. But I don’t know if that assumption is based on truth or fear.

What if I actually liked it?

What if I just need a different environment to think clearly again?

I don’t have the answer. I just know I’m no longer as certain as I used to be.

3. I’m Doing “Everything” and Still Feeling Behind

I have side projects. Scripts. Ideas. Business concepts. Creative experiments.

On paper, I’m busy.

But emotionally, I feel stuck.

I’m the kind of person who can hyperfocus on something I love—like a video game—and lose hours without even noticing. Fully engaged. Fully alive.

And I keep wondering:

Why doesn’t my work feel like that?

Why does everything feel like effort instead of flow?

Some of the projects I started were exciting at first. I genuinely wanted to build them. But somewhere along the way, the motivation faded.

Not because I don’t care—but because I don’t feel connected to them the way I thought I would.

And that makes me question something deeper:

Am I even still interested in the path I thought I wanted?

Or am I just holding onto an identity I outgrew?

The question underneath all of this

So… What Do You Do When You Feel Stuck?

That’s the real question underneath all of this.

What do you do when you feel like you’re in the wrong place in your own life?

When you know things can get better, but you have no clear map for how to get there?

When every option feels uncertain, and even staying still feels like a decision you’re not sure about?

I don’t have a clean answer.

I’m still in it.

Still figuring it out.

Still asking.

If you’re going through this too

I’m right here in it too.

If any of this resonates with you—if you’re also in a weird in-between phase of your life—I just want to say you’re not alone.

I know it can feel isolating when you’re questioning everything while everyone else seems to be moving forward with certainty.

But I’m right here in it too.

And I think a lot of us are, even if we don’t say it out loud.
