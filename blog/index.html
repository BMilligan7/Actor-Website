A Fabulous Question:
I’m a 24-Year-Old Actor—What the Hell Am I Doing With My Life?

A reflection on the struggle, the journey, the disillusion, and not knowing what comes next.

My blog topic is essentially about the struggle, the journey, the disillusion, the unhappiness, and the feeling of being overworked, lost, and confused.

I feel like I’m dying. I feel like I’m sick with the lifestyle I’m living: the constant consumption, the food, the things in the food. My body is messed up. I feel tired all the time.

And I’m doing this acting thing.

I just went to the International Black Theatre Festival, and I’m realizing that I’m not super excited, pumped, or motivated to get out there and heavily pursue acting anymore. Yes, I want to pursue it, but that’s a lot. I’m not as passionate, as driven, or as hungry for it.

Yeah, I want to be successful. Yes, I want to have fun and live my life and meet the right people and interact with the right people and look how I want to and feel attractive. But that’s part of the reason I got into acting. That’s some of the main reasons.

But now I’m questioning: is any of this shit worth it? What the fuck am I doing with my life?

I’m 24 years old, and I’m feeling lost right now. I’m currently at my parents’ house. I flew back to the East Coast for two weeks, and I’m feeling lost.

I’m a 24-year-old actor currently in my childhood bedroom at my parents’ house in my hometown. I’m sitting here in the bed, miserable, thinking about life and why there’s all this pressure to just live unhappily and work and fit into the molds that society has for us.

Acting has been something I’ve been pursuing full-time for over a year now. And it’s hard.

I think about quitting.

I’m barely making enough to pay the bills. And sometimes it’s honestly just not worth it: the stress on the body, the weight on the pockets, and the mental effect of rejection.

The International Black Theatre Festival

At the International Black Theatre Festival, I did a monologue in front of a talent agent.

I went to this theater festival with the goal of getting an agent in New York or LA before I moved there. And I thought this was gonna be it. I thought this was gonna be it.

I did a monologue that I used AI to write. And in the past, it’s been good. It’s been real grounded, real natural. I’m able to really connect with the words, and I feel the monologue.

I did it, and it didn’t feel real to the talent agent.

And it made me really think: I don’t believe myself either.

I know when I’m really acting and I’m really in my zone, and that just wasn’t it. And it made me really question my acting ability and think: am I good enough?

I’ve been doing this full-time for a year, and yet I can’t readily access that good acting that I strive for.

I left feeling disillusioned.

Now I’m at a point where I’m questioning whether I need a big change in my life—career-wise, relationship-wise, environment-wise, and energy-wise.

These are three of the main reasons why.

1. I’m out of shape, aesthetically and health-wise

I’ve been dealing with injuries recently. I’ve been an athlete my whole life. I was a bodybuilder in college and always had a good physique.

Yet today, I still have a physique that is much better than the average person. Not only am I naturally gifted, but I have a lot of muscle. However, I constantly look in the mirror every day and don’t like my appearance.

I feel like I’m fat.

And I do have a higher body-fat percentage than I’ve ever had.

Alongside that, I’m dealing with injuries left and right. And I think about why, and I think it’s really because of my habits. I’m not getting enough sleep due to stress and due to the drive to get up and wake up and go and get it. I keep putting myself in situations where they don’t allow me to rest, or where I neglect my own health.

Acting is not paying the bills.

I want to do things with my life. I want to live in different cities. I want to travel. I want to take care of my health. And in this world, I often feel the pressure that I need money to do that, even to eat right as well.

And I know that’s not true 100%, but that’s honestly how it feels.

2. I’m thinking about getting a full-time job in corporate

I’m acting full-time right now, doing mostly theater. I’ve been thinking about getting a full-time job recently in corporate under the idea that I could save up, get my money right, build a business on the side, and do that eventually—you know, how everybody else thinks.

And I’m seriously considering that because who knows? I could love that lifestyle.

I’ve never tried it, but I’ve always had this idea that I didn’t want to. So I have a curiosity to try a different mindset.

3. I’m doing a whole lot of side projects, and I still feel like I’m getting nothing done

I have multiple projects that I’m doing, from writing scripts to building a business.

I’m the type of person who fixates on things like video games. If I like a video game, I can sit there and play it for hours throughout the day, every day, and have fun doing that.

I want my work and life to be like that.

I want to be motivated and excited to get out there and play. I want that to be the most important and fun thing to me. That’s how I want my work to be.

I don’t want to do stuff because I feel like I have to do it versus because I really want to do it. That’s slavery to me.

I have all these projects that started out fun, but I haven’t been motivated to finish them because, honestly, I’ve been losing interest in them.

And that makes me question whether I’m still interested in the things that I’ve been working toward, which is primarily creating performing arts, performing artwork, and innovative products.

The question I’m sitting with

What do you do with your life when you feel stuck?

What do you do with your life when you feel stuck, like you’re in the wrong place?

When you know it’ll get better, but you don’t know how?
